---

## 🎯 Overview

**Stationery E-Commerce Website** is a fully automated, web-based platform designed exclusively for **students and teachers** to **buy and sell stationery items** (e.g., pens, notebooks, geometry boxes, old textbooks, etc.) within an academic community.

The platform promotes **reuse, reduces waste**, and provides an **affordable, secure, and user-friendly** marketplace for academic essentials.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- ✅ **User Registration & Profiles**  
  - Separate roles: Buyer & Seller  
  - Profile management (name, contact, institution)

- 🛍️ **Product Listings**  
  - Sellers can upload new or used stationery items  
  - Add title, description, price, condition, and images

- 🔍 **Smart Search & Filters**  
  - Search by name, category, or price range  
  - Filter by condition (New/Used), seller, or date

- 🛒 **Shopping Cart & Checkout**  
  - Add/remove items  
  - Cash on Delivery (COD) support  
  - Order history tracking

- 📱 **Fully Responsive Design**  
  - Works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop

- 🔒 **Secure & Fast**  
  - Authentication & authorization  
  - Optimized for quick loading
